# Privacy Policy for Files (mfile)

**Last Updated: November 6, 2026**

**mlab.software** ("we," "us," or "our") operates the **Files (mfile)** mobile application (Package Name: `com.mlab.software.mfile`). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our app and the choices you have associated with that data.

## 1. Information Collection and Use

### 1.1 Files and Storage Access
Files is an offline file manager. To browse and manage your files, the app requires access to your device's storage:
*   **Permissions Used**: `MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` (All files access), `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` (Android 12 and below), `WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` (Android 10 and below), and `READ_MEDIA_IMAGES`, `READ_MEDIA_VIDEO`, `READ_MEDIA_AUDIO` (Android 13 and above).
*   **Usage**: These permissions are used solely to list, open, view, play, search, copy, move, rename, delete, compress and extract files and folders that you choose, and to analyze storage usage on your device.
*   **Data Handling**: We do not upload your files, their contents or their metadata (names, paths, sizes, dates) to any external servers. All file processing happens entirely on your device.

### 1.2 Archives
The app can create and extract ZIP archives, including password-protected archives (AES-256). Passwords you enter are used only to perform the requested operation on your device and are not stored or transmitted.

### 1.3 External and USB Storage
If you connect a USB storage device (USB OTG) or select a storage location via the system file picker, the app accesses that storage only to display and manage its contents at your request.

### 1.4 Installed Applications
The app includes an applications manager that lists apps installed on your device (name, icon, size, version) so you can open, uninstall or locate their installation files. It also can install APK files you select.
*   **Permissions Used**: `REQUEST_INSTALL_PACKAGES` and `REQUEST_DELETE_PACKAGES`.
*   **Usage**: Installing or uninstalling an app always happens only at your explicit request and requires your confirmation in the system dialog.
*   **Data Handling**: The list of installed applications is processed only on your device and is never collected or shared.

### 1.5 User Preferences and App Data
We store your application settings (such as display preferences, favorites, folder colors and recently viewed images) locally on your device. This data is not shared with us or any third parties.

### 1.6 No Internet Access
The app does not request the `INTERNET` permission and does not transmit any data over the network. The only exception is the optional "Rate the app" prompt, which, if you choose to use it, opens the Google Play Store page of the app in the Play Store app or your browser.

## 2. Third-Party Advertising (Future Integration)

Currently, the App does not display advertisements. However, to support ongoing development, we may integrate advertising services (such as **Google AdMob**) in the future.

When advertising is integrated, third-party partners may collect information to serve relevant ads:
*   **Device Identifiers**: Such as Android Advertising ID (AAID).
*   **Analytics**: Approximate location (IP-based) and ad interaction data.
*   **Cookies/Local Storage**: Ad partners may use cookies or local storage to facilitate ad delivery and personalization.

We will update this Privacy Policy and comply with the Google Play Store's policies regarding ad disclosures and user consent (including GDPR and CCPA) at the time of integration.

## 3. Third-Party Services
The App does not use third-party analytics or crash reporting services. When you choose to rate the app, Google Play handles that interaction under Google's privacy policy: [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

## 4. Permissions Disclosure
*   **Notifications**: To display playback controls in the notification shade.
*   **Foreground Service (Media Playback)**: To allow audio playback to continue while the app is in the background.
*   **Wake Lock**: To keep audio playback from being interrupted when the screen turns off.

## 5. Data Security
Since the app does not collect or transmit your data, all your files and settings remain on your device under the protection of your device's security features. Files you share with other apps are passed through the Android system only when you explicitly choose to share or open them.

## 6. Children's Privacy
Our App does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
